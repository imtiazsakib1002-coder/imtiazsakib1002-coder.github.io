Privacy Policy for Gen Z TV

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Gen Z TV offers both a free ad-supported mode and an optional VIP login mode. For VIP accounts, login credentials are used strictly to verify active session access. We do not sell personal user data to third parties.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Gen Z TV uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP address, and usage diagnostics to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
